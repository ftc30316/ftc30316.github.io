Our team

Growing together.
Building what comes next.

Evergreen Dynamics is a community-based robotics team in Richland, Washington. We compete as team 30316 in FIRST Tech Challenge, bringing together students with different interests through a shared challenge: design, build, and program a competition robot.

We established the team in 2025, our rookie season. Our experience in FIRST LEGO League helped lay the groundwork for this next chapter. Today, strategy, design, building, code, outreach, and driving all contribute to what we create together.

We value clear communication and collaboration, both within our team and with the teams we share the field with. The goal is to keep improving, support one another, and make room for the next idea.

More than a robot

Many skills. One team.

A competition robot takes more than parts and code. These are the ways we work together to bring it to life.

Strategy

Understand the game, plan our approach, and help every match have a purpose.

Design

Explore possibilities and turn a promising idea into a mechanism we can build.

Build

Make, assemble, test, and refine the hardware that brings our design to the field.

Code

Connect software to motion, from driver control to autonomous routines.

Outreach

Share what we're learning and help others discover a place in robotics.

Drive

Practice together, communicate clearly, and put our robot and strategy to work.
